# AI Asset Depreciation

> Asset values, always audit-ready.

AI asset depreciation tracking — schedules, valuations and accounting entries, automated. Part of the **Zion AI App Network** — 550+ interlinked AI & IT tools by [Zion Tech Group](https://ziontechgroup.com).

## Features
- Automated depreciation schedules (straight-line, declining balance, MACRS, units of production)
- AI asset valuation hints from market data
- Journal entry generation for major ERPs
- Disposal, impairment and revaluation workflows
- Audit-ready reports with full history

- Close the books faster with automated depreciation runs
- Keep fixed-asset registers accurate across entities
- Produce audit evidence in one click
